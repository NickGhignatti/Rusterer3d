<h1 align="center">Rusterer3d</h1>

👋🏻 Thanks for stopping by Rusterer, my personal project where I’m diving into the world of 3D graphics
using Rust. Whether you’re here to learn, contribute, or just see some pixels get pushed around, 
you’re in the right place!

This documentation will walk you through the guts of the renderer—how it draws shapes, handles scenes,
and brings a bit of 3D magic to life. Feel free to poke around, experiment, and if you get stuck, drop an issue.
